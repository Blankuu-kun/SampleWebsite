About Us

Café Amara opened its doors in 2019, tucked into a quiet corner with just four tables and a secondhand espresso machine. What started as a weekend hobby turned into a full-time obsession with getting things right — hand-roasted beans, produce from the Saturday market, and recipes handed down from our founder's grandmother1. Every dish on this menu is made from scratch, every morning, in the kitchen behind the counter.

No shortcuts, no pre-made sauces — just food we'd actually want to eat ourselves.
